Privacy Policy (English)

LOKAI collects no personal data. What you translate, photograph, or say never leaves your device.

There is no account, no server, and no analytics, crash reporting, or advertising SDK. Translation, image recognition, speech recognition, and text-to-speech all run on the device.

Only settings are stored locally: target language, tone, interface language, onboarding state, and the daily image count for free users. No translated content is stored.

The network is used in two cases only: in-app purchase (price lookup, payment, restore — handled by Apple; the developer never receives payment details), and downloading Apple's speech recognition packs when you choose to use voice input.

No data is shared with third parties, because none is collected. Questions: kjm74053569@gmail.com
